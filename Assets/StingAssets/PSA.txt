INT. DUNGEON - NIGHT

WIZARD, ROGUE, FIGHTER, and CLERIC all stand in front of a large wooden door, staring intently at it. Wizard flips through his spell book, scanning for any spell that could help them.

Fighter walks up to the door, grabs the handle and pulls. The door remains shut.

                FIGHTER
    Well I'm out of ideas.

Rogue walks up to Fighter and pushes him aside.

                ROGUE
    Then get out of the way..

Rogue smirks while pulling out a lock pick.

                ROGUE
    This is my speciality.

Rogue fiddles with the lock. CLICK. CLICK. TINK. The lock pick falls to the floor, broken. Rogue pulls out another pick. Cleric rolls their eyes.

                CLERIC
    Let me help you this time.

Cleric holds out their hands which begin to glow with holy energy. The lock pick glows as Rogue once again attempts to open the door. CLICK. CLICK.

Wizard continues flipping through their spell book. CLICK. Wizard suddenly stops. A smirk grows on Wizard's face. TINK.

                ROGUE
    Damned lock, we'll have to find another way..

Wizard raises his hand towards the door.

                WIZARD
    FIREBALL!

Rogue, Fighter, and Cleric look back at Wizard and scream. AHHHH. BANG. The spell book falls to the ground, flipping to the next page with the Knock spell on it.

Text: Don't cast Fireball. Flip the page, save the day.
